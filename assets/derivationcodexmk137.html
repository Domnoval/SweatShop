Wiring the two new modes into the real engine

Both new traces are pure functions of the same contract your existing LINEA / CURVA / ROSETTA modes already satisfy — they take a cell sequence and return a path — so they splice in without touching the rest of MK·137.

AGRIPPA — classical name-walk: each letter → cipher value → theosophic-reduce into 1…n² → that number's cell → connect in letter order, open circle on the first node, terminal bar on the last. It's ROSETTA without the return, plus caps.

SPARE — chaos reduction: strip repeated letters first (keep first occurrence), walk only the survivors, smooth the result into a knot. Deterministic by construction — same word, same glyph, forever — which is the whole point of a meaning-compiler. The only design decision Spare left open was the knot layout; here it's the kamea cell of each survivor, so even the chaos mode derives.

Cell-selection rule: SPARE dedupes the letter set; every other mode walks the full name. Line style is orthogonal: straight (LINEA) · smoothed (CURVA/SPARE/AGRIPPA) · smoothed-and-closed (ROSETTA). Drop the two cases into your trace switch and the gallery becomes a superset of the Televisor.

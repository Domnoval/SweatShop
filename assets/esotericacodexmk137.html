Honest seams

The angelic, Olympic, and Goetic seals are generated — deterministic glyphs standing in for the historical plates, not photographic copies. The authentic move is to derive them: spirits and intelligences are kamea-walks of their names, and that lives in the companion MK·137 instrument. Swap in a manuscript plate any time you want fidelity over derivation.

The Adinkra, veve, and Rose-Cross renders are reduced to their essential geometry — the originals carry far more line-work. Vodou veve are living liturgical drawings; these are respectful diagrams, not ritual objects.

Unicode blocks render through Noto webfonts so the real characters appear even where the OS lacks them — the one external dependency, and it's load-bearing: without it the esoterica is tofu.
